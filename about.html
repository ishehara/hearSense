Our Team

Team Overview

Our multidisciplinary team consists of experts in machine learning, computer vision, software development, UI/UX design, and accessibility. We are united by a common mission: to bridge communication gaps for deaf and hard-of-hearing individuals through innovative technology.

Each team member brings unique expertise and a deep commitment to creating practical solutions that make a real difference in people's lives.
