How to Access Presentations

Tips for viewing and downloading

Step 1: Browse Presentations

Scroll through the presentations list above to find the presentation you're interested in. Each presentation includes a description and date information.

Step 2: Download Files

Click the "Download" button to download the presentation file (typically in PPTX or PDF format) to your computer.

Step 3: View Online

Alternatively, click "View" to open the presentation directly in your browser without downloading.

Step 4: Required Software

To view PPTX files, you'll need PowerPoint, LibreOffice, Google Slides, or a compatible viewer. PDF files can be viewed with any PDF reader.

Step 5: Recommended Viewing Order

Start with the Proposal, then Progress 1 and 2, and finish with the Final presentation for a complete project journey.
