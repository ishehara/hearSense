Component 02

🔊 Sound Alert System

A background listening system that detects dangerous sounds in the environment and immediately alerts deaf and hearing-impaired users through vivid visual notifications

← Back to Home

What Is This Component?

AI-powered sound awareness for deaf and hearing-impaired users

The Sound Alert component is a background listening system designed specifically for deaf and hearing-impaired users. It runs on the user's mobile phone and continuously listens to the surrounding environment through the phone microphone every 2.5 seconds.

When it detects a dangerous or important sound nearby, it immediately alerts the user through the phone screen so they are aware of what is happening around them — even if they cannot hear it.

What Problem Does It Solve?

Deaf individuals face serious everyday safety risks they cannot hear

⚠️ The Dangers

  • Walking on the road when a car is honking to warn them
  • Not realizing an ambulance or fire truck is approaching
  • Being unaware of a police siren nearby
  • Missing warning sounds from trucks, buses, or trains

🛡️ The Solution

This component acts as the "ears" of the application. It uses AI to identify critical sounds and alerts the user through clear visual notifications — making safety automatic, not accidental.

  • Continuous 2.5-second listening cycle
  • Instant on-screen visual alerts
  • Tiered priority based on danger level
  • Full alert history for review

What Sounds Does It Detect?

Seven critical sound types across two categories

🚗 Vehicle Horns

🚗 Car Horn
🚌 Bus Horn
🚛 Truck Horn
🚂 Train Horn

🚨 Emergency Sirens

🚑 Ambulance Siren
🚒 Fire Truck Siren
🚓 Police Siren

Each detection produces its own specific alert with a matching icon so the user knows exactly what was heard.

How Does It Work in Real Life?

Simple for the user, powerful under the hood

1

Start Monitoring

The user opens the Sound Alert screen and taps the Start Monitoring button to begin.

2

Continuous Listening

The phone microphone automatically listens to surroundings every 2.5 seconds in the background.

3

Sound Detected

When a horn or siren is detected with sufficient confidence, the app immediately triggers a visual alert.

4

User Alerted

The user sees the alert and becomes aware of the danger in their environment.

5

Alert History Saved

All detections are saved to an Alert History so the user can review what sounds occurred throughout the day.

📍 Real-Life Scenario

A deaf person is walking near a road. An ambulance approaches from behind with its siren on. The app detects the siren through the phone microphone. The entire phone screen turns red and shows:

🚑
"Ambulance Detected"
HIGH PRIORITY

The phone vibrates continuously until the user taps DISMISS. The user sees it, steps aside, and stays safe.

Notification Priority System

Not all sounds are equally dangerous — the app responds accordingly

TIER 1 — EMERGENCY

Full-Screen Red Alert

Must be dismissed manually. Triggered by the most dangerous sounds:

🚑 Ambulance Siren
🚒 Fire Truck Siren
🚓 Police Siren
🚂 Train Horn
🚛 Truck Horn
🚌 Bus Horn

On-screen effects:

  • Entire screen turns red
  • Large emoji and sound name in the centre
  • Continuous strong vibration pattern
  • Screen flashing
  • User must tap DISMISS to clear
TIER 2 — WARNING

Auto-Dismiss Banner

Disappears after 3.5 seconds. Triggered by less urgent sounds:

🚗 Car Horn
🏍️ Motorcycle Horn

On-screen effects:

  • Banner slides down from the top
  • Shows sound name
  • Light vibration
  • Disappears automatically after a few seconds
SILENT / AMBIENT

No Visual Alert

Background traffic noise is logged silently in history but does not show any banner or interrupt the user — too frequent, not immediately dangerous.

Priority rule:

  • Emergency alerts always take priority over warnings
  • Tier 1 covers any simultaneously received banner

User settings:

  • Individual sound types can be toggled on/off
  • Emergency sirens are always active — cannot be disabled

Screens in the App

Three dedicated screens for full sound awareness control

🏠 Home (Sound Monitoring)

  • Start / Stop monitoring button
  • Live list of recent alerts with time, type, and severity
  • Filter alerts by severity (All / High / Medium / Low)
  • Backend connection status indicator

📋 Alert History

  • Full history grouped by Today / Yesterday / This Week
  • Filter by sound type or severity
  • Review what sounds occurred throughout the day

⚙️ Settings

  • Toggle individual horn types on or off
  • Toggle vibration, screen flash, and banner notifications
  • Emergency sirens are always on — cannot be disabled

Who Is It For?

Designed for safety-critical everyday use

👂 Deaf and hearing-impaired individuals in Sri Lanka
📱 Users of the Intelligent Sinhala Sign Language app
🛡️ Anyone needing sound-awareness assistance in daily life

Overall Purpose

The Sound Alert component gives hearing-impaired users the ability to be aware of dangerous sounds in their environment in real time. It acts as a safety layer using the phone's microphone and artificial intelligence to recognize threatening sounds and immediately notify the user through clear visual alerts — helping them stay safe in everyday situations that hearing people handle automatically through sound.
