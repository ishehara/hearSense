🎓 Adaptive Learning System

An intelligent, personalized sign language training module that uses real-time AI to teach Sinhala Sign Language with adaptive feedback and progress tracking

← Back to Home

What Is This Component?

Personalized AI-powered Sinhala Sign Language training on your mobile phone

The Adaptive Learning System is an intelligent, personalized sign language training module designed specifically for learners of Sinhala Sign Language (SSL). It runs on the user's mobile phone and uses real-time camera input combined with artificial intelligence to teach users how to correctly perform Sinhala sign letters.

When the user performs a sign in front of the camera, the system instantly detects the hand gesture, analyzes its correctness, and provides personalized feedback. Unlike static learning materials, this component learns from each user's performance and continuously adapts the learning path to match their unique learning pace and style — making every user's learning experience different and optimized for maximum retention.

What Problem Does It Solve?

Traditional sign language learning has critical limitations

❌ The Problems

  • Fixed curriculum that doesn't adapt to learner needs
  • Memorizing signs without real-time feedback or correction
  • No way to verify if you're performing a sign correctly
  • Losing motivation because progress feels slow or repetitive
  • Spending time on signs you already know
  • No record of improvement over time
  • Generic feedback that doesn't help YOU specifically improve

✅ The Solutions

  • Real-time gesture recognition and validation
  • Instant, personalized feedback tailored to each learner
  • Adaptive curriculum that adjusts difficulty to skill level
  • Spaced repetition that reminds you to review weak signs
  • AI-powered recommendations about what to practice next
  • Detailed performance tracking and progress visualization

How Does It Work?

A step-by-step real-life learning flow

1

Choose a Sign

The system recommends a sign based on your progress — a weak sign needing practice, the next recommended one, or any sign you choose.

2

Perform & Detect

Perform the sign in front of the camera. The system detects your hand position, analyzes the gesture, and generates a confidence score (0–100%).

3

Get Instant Feedback

Receive instant feedback: ✅ Correct (≥55% confidence), ⚠️ Try Again (45–55%), ❌ Incorrect, or 💡 Correction Tip showing what to adjust.

4

All Attempts Logged

Every attempt is recorded with accuracy, confidence level, time taken, and feedback given. The system builds a detailed performance history.

5

Pattern Analysis

Signs you struggle with are recommended more frequently. Signs you've mastered come up less often. Optimal review timing is calculated automatically.

6

Personalized Sessions

The next session greets you with personalized suggestions, your current streak, signs due for review, and your overall progress toward mastery.

Intelligent Feedback Mechanisms

Three AI agents compete to give you the best possible feedback

🤖

Reinforcement Learning Agent

Basic Model

Learns which feedback messages historically help users improve. Tracks what types of hints lead to faster mastery. Adapts feedback over time based on your response patterns.

📚

Adaptive Curriculum Agent

Advanced Model

Maintains spaced repetition schedules for each sign. Tracks mastery progression, recommends optimal learning sequences, and adjusts difficulty based on your current accuracy.

📊

Thompson Sampling Agent

Statistical Model

Uses Bayesian statistics to find the BEST feedback for each individual user. Learns whether you respond better to encouragement ("You're close!") or technical guidance ("Bend your thumb 15 degrees").

Result: Each user gets feedback optimized specifically for their learning style — not generic messages that work for everyone.

Mastery Tracking System

Comprehensive performance metrics at every level

📌 Per-Sign Metrics

  • Attempt Count — how many times you've tried
  • Accuracy — % of correct attempts
  • Avg Confidence — average across all attempts
  • Best Performance — highest confidence ever achieved
  • Current Streak — consecutive correct attempts
  • Longest Streak — best streak ever for that sign
  • Mastery Level — 0 (not started) → 4 (expert)
  • Review Status — due for spaced repetition?
  • Days Since Practice — last practice date

📈 Overall Progress

  • Total Attempts — sum of all practice attempts
  • Overall Accuracy — average across all signs
  • Avg Confidence — overall confidence level
  • Signs Attempted — different signs tried
  • Signs Familiar — mastery level ≥ 2
  • Signs Mastered — mastery level ≥ 3
  • Current Level — which curriculum level you're on
  • Progress % — how far toward complete mastery

💾 Data Persistence

  • Every attempt logged with timestamp and result
  • Persists across app sessions and devices
  • AI agents learn YOUR individual patterns
  • Carries preferred feedback style across sessions
  • Optimal learning pace tracked over time
  • Synchronizes across devices when logged in

The system gets SMARTER the more you use it.
