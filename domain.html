Research & Domain Analysis

Comprehensive research background and technical foundation

Literature Survey

Understanding the current landscape of sign language technology

Sign Language Recognition

Extensive research exists in the field of sign language recognition using computer vision and deep learning. Key methodologies include:

  • Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN)
  • Recurrent Neural Networks (RNN)
  • Pose estimation techniques
  • Hand keypoint detection

Assistive Technology for Deaf

Multiple studies have explored assistive technologies designed specifically for deaf and hard-of-hearing individuals:

  • Visual notification systems
  • Real-time captioning
  • Gesture recognition interfaces
  • Accessibility standards

Audio Processing & Detection

Advanced audio analysis techniques for critical sound detection and environmental awareness:

  • Spectrogram analysis
  • Sound classification ML models
  • Real-time audio streaming
  • Emergency sound detection

Communication Accessibility

Research on creating accessible communication platforms for individuals with disabilities:

  • AAC (Augmentative and Alternative Communication)
  • Predictive text systems
  • Multi-modal interfaces
  • User experience design

Research Problem

Identified Challenges

The deaf and hard-of-hearing population faces significant communication barriers in daily life:

  • Communication Gap: Limited effective communication tools between sign language users and hearing individuals
  • Safety Concerns: Inability to detect critical sounds (emergency sirens, doorbells, alarms)
  • Education Barriers: Lack of personalized sign language learning systems
  • Social Inclusion: Limited access to inclusive communication platforms
  • Language Specificity: Insufficient resources for Sinhala Sign Language specifically

Research Gap

Opportunities for innovation and improvement

🎯 Limited SSL Technology

Sinhala Sign Language technology is underrepresented in current research compared to ASL and other major sign languages. There's a need for SSL-specific models and datasets.

🔗 Integrated Solutions

Existing solutions address individual problems separately. An integrated platform combining learning, recognition, and communication is lacking.

👨‍👧 Child-Specific Systems

Limited solutions specifically designed for children with speaking disabilities and their family communication needs.

🌍 Real-World Accessibility

Gap between academic research and practical, user-friendly applications accessible to the deaf community.
