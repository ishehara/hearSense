Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions

HearSense is an intelligent platform combining AI, computer vision, and audio processing to provide comprehensive communication solutions for deaf and hard-of-hearing individuals. It includes sign language recognition, learning systems, and safety alerts.
HearSense can be accessed through web browsers and mobile applications. The platform provides four main features: adaptive learning for sign language, sound detection alerts, real-time sign language recognition, and two-way communication tools. Visit our documentation for detailed usage instructions.
The platform is currently in the research and development phase. We are committed to making it accessible to the deaf community. Pricing and availability will be determined based on user needs and community feedback. Contact us for more information.
We welcome collaboration and contributions from researchers, developers, and members of the deaf community. If you're interested in contributing, please reach out to us through the contact form or email us at info@hearsense.lk.
HearSense uses cutting-edge technologies including TensorFlow/PyTorch for machine learning, OpenCV and MediaPipe for computer vision, Python for backend development, and modern web technologies for the frontend. See the Domain section for complete technical details.
You can report bugs or request features using our contact form above. Select "Bug Report" or "Feature Request" as the category and provide detailed information. Alternatively, email us at support@hearsense.lk with your feedback.
